Common questions

Does Scrapborn need an internet connection?
No. The whole game runs on your device. A connection is only used to load a rewarded ad if you choose to watch one, and to complete a purchase.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device. There is no account and no server, so deleting the app deletes your save.
I bought Supporter and the ads came back.
Open the garage, tap SUPPORTER, and choose RESTORE PURCHASE. That asks the App Store to re-confirm your purchase on this device. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on. If it still does not restore, email us and say which country your Apple Account is in.
Am I forced to watch ads?
No. Every ad in Scrapborn is a rewarded ad you opt into — to get back up after a boss kills you, to recover salvage you dropped, or to run a salvage job from the garage. Nothing is gated behind watching one.
Are the ads personalised to me?
No. Scrapborn never asks for tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier, so ads are served contextually. In regions where a consent form is shown, you can change your answer at any time: open the garage, tap SUPPORTER (or FINISHES, if you already bought it), and choose AD PRIVACY SETTINGS.
The game runs hot or drops frames.
Scrapborn renders a lot of machines at once. Closing other apps helps. Please email us your device model if it is unplayable — that is a bug we want.
