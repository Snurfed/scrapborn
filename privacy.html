Scrapborn Privacy Policy

Snurfed LLC — last updated 5 November 2026

Scrapborn is a single-player game that runs on your device. This policy describes everything it does with data. It is short because the app does very little.

What we collect

Nothing. Snurfed LLC operates no servers for Scrapborn, runs no analytics or telemetry, and receives no information about you or your play. There is no account and no sign-in.

Your save data

Your progress — frames, modules, materials, blueprints and settings — is stored in a file inside the app's own container on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Scrapborn shows rewarded ads only, and only when you choose to watch one: to get back up after a boss defeats you, to recover salvage you dropped, or to run a salvage job from the garage. Nothing in the game is locked behind watching an ad, and no ad is ever shown without you asking for it.

These ads are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When you request an ad, that SDK contacts Google and may process device and connection information in order to select, deliver and measure the ad, and to detect fraud. That processing is Google's, under Google's privacy policy; see also how Google uses data from sites and apps that use their services.

Scrapborn does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not share anything with data brokers. Ads are served contextually rather than personalised to you.

Where the law requires it — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time: open the garage, tap SUPPORTER (or FINISHES, if you already bought it), and choose AD PRIVACY SETTINGS.

Purchases

The one-time Supporter purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Snurfed LLC never sees or stores your payment details, your name, or your Apple Account. The app only learns whether the purchase is currently valid on this device.

Children

Scrapborn is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, email us and we will act on it.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. To remove everything Scrapborn has stored, delete the app. Advertising choices are handled through the consent form described above and through Settings › Privacy & Security on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
